## Emphasis

```markdown
*italic*, **bold**, ***both***, ~~struck~~, ==highlighted==, ++inserted++, `code`
```

```markdown
H~2~O and E = mc^2^
```

## Smart typography

Quotes, dashes and ellipses are converted as you type them (`features.typographer`):

```markdown
"double" and 'single' quotes, en -- dash, em --- dash, wait...
```

"double" and 'single' quotes, en -- dash, em --- dash, wait...
